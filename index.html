Frequently Asked Questions

What are your Minimum Order Quantities (MOQ)?

Our standard MOQ is 25-50 units for executive gift hampers, 50 units for drinkware & corporate polo shirts, and 100 units for die-struck metal badges.

Can we review a physical sample before bulk manufacturing?

Yes! For all corporate orders exceeding 200 units, we fabricate and deliver a complimentary pre-production physical prototype for your leadership to touch and verify before mass runs.

Do you provide official FBR sales tax invoices?

Absolutely. Circle One is an active FBR taxpayer with registered NTN (9481203-7) and SRB credentials. All shipments are accompanied by official tax invoices.

What are your standard delivery turnaround timelines?

For Karachi corporate clients, standard delivery is 3 to 7 business days following sample approval. For nationwide deliveries (Lahore, Islamabad, Faisalabad, etc.), timeline is 7 to 10 business days.
